## Context

`0.6.0` is merged into `dev`. The `0.7.0` slice changes the floor from a
seeded four-room linear chain to a fixed six-room branch, replaces the
automatic full-spirit Warden summon with an explicit arena interaction, and
adds run-scoped skill points, a safe-room shop, a healing draught, Vigor, and
the corresponding HUD/feedback.

These are structural changes, not a presentation-only pass. QA must verify
the new economy and shop rules, the branch topology, the explicit summon,
state persistence/reset, and regressions in the existing combat,
purification, boss, death, pause, restart, descent, audio, and boot flows.

## Objective

Independently verify that the implemented `0.7.0` reward loop matches
`docs/tasks/0.7.0/001-programmer.md` and
`design/0.7.0/design-decisions.md`, that every approved rule and edge case is
observable, and that existing behaviour has not regressed.

## In scope

- Fixed six-room branch topology, traversal, shop dead end, arena gate,
  summon point, and stairs across multiple seeds.
- Arena lock/unlock and the absence of automatic summoning.
- Explicit Warden summon, boss fight, boss defeat, stairs, and descent.
- Skill-point awards, HUD updates, persistence across descent, and reset on
  death/restart.
- Shop availability, inventory, prices, purchase success/failure, draught
  carrying/use/healing, Vigor one-time purchase, and shop closure after
  summon.
- Shop safety and the ability to shop without taking damage.
- HUD skill-point and draught indicators, heart-row adaptation at raised
  maximum HP, controls, feedback, and accessibility.
- Unit-test quality and coverage for the new rules; Cypress coverage for the
  full flow and edge cases.
- Existing suite regressions and updates required by the new topology and
  explicit summon.
- Build, typecheck, unit, e2e, production-bundle, dependency, version, and
  diff-scope checks.
- Design compliance and visual verification at 1024 × 640 and 1440 × 810.

## Out of scope

- Subjective art review beyond the approved design decisions.
- Gameplay balance tuning or new feature requests.
- Hosted CI execution; GitHub owns that verification.
- Vercel deployment verification.
- Mobile/touch layout, remapping, settings, localisation, or persistence.
- Post-death purchases, meta-progression, difficulty scaling, or a general
  dungeon generator.

### Edge-case checks

- Exactly enough points for a purchase succeeds and leaves 0.
- One point short fails with no deduction.
- Buying a draught at full HP is allowed; using it at full HP is blocked and
  does not consume it (default).
- Using a draught at 1 HP below maximum heals 1 and consumes it.
- Buying a draught while carrying one is blocked; using the carried draught
  first allows another purchase if points remain.
- Vigor at full HP raises max and current HP by 1 (default).
- Vigor purchased on floor 1 is absent on floor 2 and later.
- A carried draught survives descent and shows as carried in the next shop.
- A failed rite, conventional kill, or respawn grants no points.
- Resting the last mob awards 1 point, fills spirit, and unlocks the arena
  without summoning the Warden.
- The gate and stairs cannot be bypassed by walking around them.
- The fixed branch is traversable across multiple seeds with no room
  overlaps or unintended dead ends.

### Regression checks

## Approved behaviour under verification

The verification plan treats the following as the approved contract:

1. Each floor is a fixed six-room branch: start → first mob → junction;
   left → second mob → shop dead end; right → third mob → gated boss arena.
2. Resting all three mobs fills the spirit meter and unlocks the arena. The
   Warden is summoned only by an explicit interaction inside the unlocked
   arena; there is no automatic summon.
3. The player may shop before summoning. The shop closes for the floor once
   the Warden is summoned.
4. Skill points are the only currency: 1 per rested mob, 3 for the Warden,
   run-scoped across floors, and reset on death/restart.
5. The shop offers a healing draught (2 HP for 1 point, one carried slot) and
   Vigor (+1 maximum HP for 3 points, one-time per run; absent from later
   shops after purchase).
6. One draught is carried at a time; a dedicated key uses it; healing is
   capped at maximum HP; buying another while carrying is blocked.
7. The shop room is safe from enemy damage. The arena gate and stairs cannot
   be bypassed. Rewards are shop-only.
8. The HUD shows skill points and carried-draught state and adapts to raised
   maximum HP. Feedback and accessibility follow the approved design.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `npm ci`, `npm run typecheck`, `npm test`, `npm run build`, and
   `CYPRESS_BROWSER=chrome npm run e2e` pass on the tested commit.
2. The floor has the approved fixed six-room branch topology on multiple
   seeds, is fully traversable, and the shop is a one-entrance dead end.
3. The arena is locked until all three mobs are rested; the gate opens on
   purification and cannot be bypassed; no automatic Warden summon occurs.
4. The player can enter the unlocked arena and summon the Warden explicitly,
   or backtrack to the shop first. The summon guards and repeat-summon
   behaviour match the task.
5. Skill points award 1 per rested mob and 3 for the Warden, show in the
   HUD, persist across descent, and reset on death/restart. No other
   currency exists.
6. The shop offers the draught for 1 point and Vigor for 3 points with the
   approved effects. Purchase, blocked purchase, use, healing cap, and
   one-time Vigor behaviour match the task.
7. The shop closes for the floor when the Warden is summoned; later shops
   reopen but omit purchased Vigor.
8. The shop is safe: no enemy can enter it or damage the player inside it.
9. The HUD shows skill points and carried-draught state, adapts the heart row
   to raised maximum HP, and remains readable at 1024 × 640 and 1440 × 810.
10. The control legend and README document the new controls; feedback and
    accessibility decisions are implemented.
11. Run-scoped state persists/reset correctly across descent, death, and
    restart; per-floor spirit, purification, boss, gate, and shop-closure
    state reset correctly.
12. The new unit tests cover floor topology, economy awards, purchase/heal
    rules, explicit summon, gate/stairs, persistence, and reset.
13. The new Cypress coverage exercises the full earn → traverse → buy/use →
    summon → boss → descend flow with real actions, plus the specified edge
    cases.
14. Existing title, loading, combat, purification, boss, death, pause,
    restart, and descent coverage passes without weakened assertions.
15. The production bundle has no debug hooks, no new runtime dependency, and
    no external request; `package.json` is `0.7.0`.
16. The diff contains only approved `0.7.0` scope; no new art/audio,
    post-death purchases, meta-progression, persistence, difficulty scaling,
    or general dungeon generator is introduced.
17. Visual verification at 1024 × 640 and 1440 × 810 confirms the branch
    layout, gate states, shop states, HUD, summon, boss, and shop closure
    match the approved design decisions.
18. Every finding has severity, reproduction, expected vs actual, evidence,
    owner, and required rerun. The QA report records a `Passed`,
    `Changes requested`, or `Blocked` verdict with residual risks.
