## 2. Context

This is a combat-integrity hotfix, not new content. The failure modes were
found by manual play after the `0.9.0` MVP confirmation. QA must verify the
rules independently, not just re-run the programmer's tests.

The acceptance contract is `GOAL.md` SC1–SC10 as amended on 2026-11-15: SC2
now includes the mob HP reset. The design contract is
`design/0.9.1/design-decisions.md`.

## 3. Objective

Verify that a hit resets the rite completely, every distinct enemy attack
counts, mobs cannot stack, and no existing loop regressed.

## 4. Required evidence

### Rite reset

1. Break one or both fragments on a mob, damage its HP, then take a melee hit.
   All fragments return, HP returns to full, and a `rite-reset` event is
   emitted. Repeating with a projectile and with a bomb gives the same result.
2. Break fragments, damage HP, then dodge the hit. Nothing resets and the
   player takes no damage.
3. Damage the Warden, take a hit. The Warden's HP is unchanged.
4. Kill a mob conventionally while fragments remain; it still respawns after
   the configured delay with full HP and a fresh rite.
5. Rest a mob normally; the spirit and skill-point awards still occur, and the
   arena still unlocks after all three mobs rest.

### Damage attribution

6. Put two or three living melee mobs in range with their attacks resolving in
   the same step. Each enemy's damage is applied; the player loses the sum, not
   one hit's worth.
7. Let the same enemy's active window span several frames. It still deals
   damage only once.
8. Dodge through an enemy attack. No damage, no rite reset.

### Separation

9. Lure two or three mobs toward the player and observe them over several
   seconds. Their bodies remain distinct; no two living mobs occupy the same
   point, and they do not read as one combatant.
10. Verify the Warden is not moved by separation and that dead/dying bodies do
    not block living mobs.
11. Check separation near a wall, the arena gate, and the shop safe zone; no
    enemy is pushed through geometry or into the safe zone.

### Regression

12. Run the full loop: title → fight/rest → shop purchase → summon Warden →
    defeat → descend → death/restart.
13. Confirm the safe-room ward and shop behavior from `0.9.0` still work.
14. Confirm no new HUD, copy, hotkey, audio, or debug hook in production.

### Commands

15. From a clean install: `npm run typecheck`, `npm test`, `npm run build`,
    `npm run e2e`. Record counts and runtimes. Confirm the production bundle has
    no `__gameDebug`.
16. Record hosted CI as pending until visible on GitHub. Do not claim a hosted
    run passed locally.

## 5. Acceptance criteria

- All required evidence above is recorded with pass/fail and reproduction notes.
- No blocking finding remains.
- The only acceptable non-blocking findings are evidence-size limitations,
  timing variance, or deferred polish explicitly outside this hotfix.
- The QA verdict is one of `Passed`, `Passed after fixes`, or `Failed`.
- The report links the commit tested and the exact commands run.

## 6. Out of scope

- New content, balance redesign, or AI improvements beyond the approved rules.
- Changing the design contract or the product scope.
- Re-running the entire `0.9.0` acceptance matrix unless a regression requires
  it.

## 7. Dependencies and handoff

Do not start until the programmer handoff is committed. If a rule is ambiguous,
stop and ask the orchestrator; do not silently choose a behavior.

## 8. Open assumptions / unresolved items

None. The operator approved every-hit damage and the rite reset on 2026-11-15.
