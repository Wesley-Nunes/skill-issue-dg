## Context

`0.1.0` is the first playable slice: one hand-authored room, one player, one melee enemy, movement, aiming, attack, dodge, HP, death, and restart. QA verifies the implementation against the programmer task and the approved design, independently of the programmer's own claims.

## Objective

Produce an evidence-backed pass or a list of actionable blocking findings for the playable combat slice.

## In scope

- Automated checks: install, typecheck, unit tests, build, and Cypress e2e using the documented scripts.
- Functional verification of every acceptance criterion in `001-programmer.md`.
- Design compliance against every concrete value and state in `design/0.1.0/design-decisions.md`.
- Scope check against the task's "Out of scope" list.
- Manual browser verification of feel-critical behavior: responsiveness, collision, aim mapping, camera, hit/windup readability, death/restart.

## Out of scope

- Combo, consumables, skill points, shop, boss, procedural generation, persistence, audio, and menu flows (not implemented in this version).
- Performance benchmarking beyond obvious frame-rate problems on the test machine.
- Fixing defects directly. QA reports and routes findings to the responsible agent.
- Approving deviations that were not approved by the orchestrator or design.

## Approved behavior to verify

All acceptance criteria in `docs/tasks/0.1.0/001-programmer.md` plus:

- Attack resolves in the mouse aim direction with no auto-targeting.
- Domain rules are pure and covered by unit tests; rendering/input never decide outcomes.
- Dodge and damage invulnerability match the approved design values.
- The enemy cannot pass through walls and stops dealing damage when dead.
- Restart restores the exact initial state rather than a partially reset one.
- No out-of-scope system or dependency was added.

## Test scenarios and required evidence

### A. Automated

1. Fresh install succeeds from a clean `node_modules`.
2. `typecheck`, `test`, `build`, and `e2e` pass; record exact commands and results.
3. Confirm the production build contains the expected public assets and no source-map/debug regression that breaks loading.

### B. Boot and presentation

4. Load the dev server in a desktop browser: canvas fills the designed viewport, pixel art is crisp, room/player/enemy/HUD match the design layout.
5. Resize the window and verify scaling, pointer aiming, and camera remain correct.
6. Verify no uncaught console errors during boot, play, death, and restart.

### C. Movement and collision

7. WASD in all eight directions; diagonal speed equals cardinal speed.
8. Walk into every wall and each room corner; the player stops and slides without entering or tunneling.
9. Dodge toward and along walls; the player never leaves the room.
10. Hold movement and attack simultaneously; movement remains responsive.

### D. Aim, attack, combat

11. Aim at the enemy from outside and inside range; the aim indicator and attack direction agree with the cursor.
12. Attack from behind the enemy's facing: still hits if inside the approved arc; verify the arc boundary.
13. Verify one hit per swing per enemy and cooldown gating.
14. Verify windup → active → recovery timing and hit feedback against the design.
15. Verify enemy pursuit, wall avoidance, attack telegraph, attack range, and cooldown.
16. Verify enemy death removes it and stops all damage.

### E. Health, dodge, death, restart

17. Take hits until death: HP decrements one heart step at a time per approved values, invulnerability prevents multi-hit on a single contact, HUD matches state.
18. Dodge through an enemy attack during the invulnerability window: no damage; immediately after the window: damage.
19. Confirm dodge cooldown and inability to dodge while on cooldown.
20. Kill the enemy, then die; restart restores the enemy and full initial state.
21. Confirm gameplay is frozen after death and only restart input is accepted.
22. Restart repeatedly; no duplicated entities, stale timers, or growing state.

### F. Scope and architecture

23. Inspect the diff: no out-of-scope features, no extra dependencies, no asset modifications, and the `src/` layout follows the documented responsibilities.
24. Spot-check that pure systems contain no DOM/Canvas access and that rendering/input do not compute hits or death.
25. Confirm the programmer decision log documents tuning values and any deviation.

## Acceptance criteria

QA records a **pass** only if:

- every automated check passes;
- every scenario above is executed and its result recorded;
- all design-specified values and states are present, or each mismatch is either fixed or explicitly approved in writing;
- no out-of-scope feature, dependency, or asset change exists;
- no blocking finding remains open.

Otherwise QA records **changes requested** with reproducible steps, expected versus actual behavior, severity, and the responsible agent for each finding.

## Dependencies and handoff

Inputs: programmer implementation commit, approved design decisions, programmer decision log, copied assets.

Outputs: `qa/0.1.0/001-report.md` containing environment, commands, scenario results, screenshots/GIF references, findings, and a final pass/changes-requested verdict. Then hand back to the orchestrator.

## Open assumptions / unresolved items

- Test browser: the same desktop browser used by the programmer; record exact version in the report.
- Manual verification is allowed for feel and visual feedback where automation would be brittle; automated checks remain mandatory.
- Performance expectations are unset for this version; report only frame-rate problems that make the slice unplayable.
