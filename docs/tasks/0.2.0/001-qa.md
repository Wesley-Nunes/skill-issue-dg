## Context

`0.2.0` adds combat depth to the shipped `0.1.0` slice: a three-hit combo chain, a kiting ranged enemy with a projectile, and one earn-only combo challenge shown in the HUD. The room now holds two melee enemies and one ranged enemy, and clearing the room does not end the run. QA verifies the implementation independently against this task and the approved design, re-running the `0.1.0` regressions as well.

## Objective

Produce an evidence-backed pass or a list of actionable blocking findings for the combat-depth version.

## In scope

- Automated checks on a clean install: typecheck, unit tests, build, Cypress e2e using the documented scripts.
- Functional verification of every acceptance criterion in `001-programmer.md`.
- Design compliance against every concrete value and state in `design/0.2.0/design-decisions.md`.
- Regression verification that `0.1.0` behaviour still holds: movement, collision, aim, camera, dodge, HP/i-frames, melee enemy, death, restart.
- Manual browser verification of feel-critical behaviour: combo chaining and reset, finisher impact, ranged spacing and telegraph readability, projectile dodging, HUD award notice.
- Scope check against the task's "Out of scope" list and architecture check against `docs/about-the-project.md` §8.

## Out of scope

- Secondary attack, skills, skill-point spending, shop, consumables, currency, boss, procedural generation, persistence, audio, and menu flows (not implemented in this version).
- Performance benchmarking beyond obvious frame-rate problems on the test machine.
- Fixing defects directly. QA reports and routes findings to the responsible agent.
- Approving deviations that were not approved by the orchestrator or design.

## Approved behavior to verify

All acceptance criteria in `docs/tasks/0.2.0/001-programmer.md`, plus:

- Three-hit chain: 2 / 2 / 4 damage, wider finisher arc, stagger, ~0.30 s window, buffered input, whiff or timeout ends the chain.
- Ranged enemy: near/far band values, line-of-sight gating, telegraph, projectile speed, 1 damage, wall destruction.
- Fixed 2 melee + 1 ranged composition; room clears when all die; no respawn; clearing does not win.
- 1 skill point on the first fully connected chain of a run; HUD counter; lost on death; cleared by `R`.
- Projectiles damage only the player and never enemies.
- Domain rules remain pure and covered by unit tests; rendering and input never decide outcomes.

## Test scenarios and required evidence

### A. Automated

1. Fresh install succeeds from a clean `node_modules`.
2. `typecheck`, `test`, `build`, and `e2e` pass; record exact commands, counts, and results.
3. Confirm the production build contains the `imp` frames, no missing asset requests, and no debug hook in the normal production bundle.

### B. Boot and presentation

4. Boot shows the same room and HUD plus the skill-point counter at rest (0 points) and three enemies at the designed spawns.
5. Resize the window; scaling, pointer aiming, and camera remain correct with the extra entities.
6. No uncaught console errors during boot, play, combo, death, and restart.

### C. Combo chain

7. Land a full chain on an enemy: verify three separate hits with 2 / 2 / 4 damage and the finisher's stagger.
8. Press attack inside the chain window and confirm the chain advances; press after the window and confirm it restarts at step one.
9. Whiff a step and confirm the chain ends; confirm input buffering during a swing produces the next step, not a duplicate of the current one.
10. Position two enemies inside one swing and confirm both take damage exactly once from that step.
11. Confirm dodge during a swing cancels it and ends the chain per the design.
12. Confirm the chain window is visible or otherwise readable, and that the reset feedback appears.

### D. Ranged enemy and projectile

13. Approach the ranged enemy from inside the near band: it retreats without entering walls. Move beyond the far band: it closes. Hold mid-band: it holds and fires.
14. Break line of sight with a wall corner: it does not fire until sight is restored.
15. Observe the telegraph, then the projectile; verify its speed, travel, and that it is destroyed on a wall without passing through it.
16. Take a projectile hit: exactly 1 damage, invulnerability respected, HUD matches. Dodge through a projectile: no damage.
17. Confirm projectiles never damage enemies.
18. Kill the ranged enemy: it plays the approved removal and stops firing; no orphan projectiles persist as damaging objects.

### E. Challenge, HUD, room clear

19. Land a full chain: exactly 1 point is awarded, the HUD updates, and the earn notice appears once.
20. Land further full chains in the same run: no additional points.
21. Die and restart: the counter returns to 0 and the challenge can be earned again after `R`.
22. Kill all three enemies: the room-clear state is set, nothing respawns, and the run continues (player still moves, attacks, dies).

### F. Death, restart, regressions

23. Death freezes gameplay including projectiles and telegraphs; only `R` is accepted.
24. Repeated restarts restore exactly three enemies at their spawns, zero projectiles, step-one combo state, zero points, and full player HP.
25. Re-run the `0.1.0` behaviour checks: movement and sliding, diagonal speed, aim mapping, camera clamp, dodge distance/cooldown/i-frames, melee telegraph and cooldown, hearts, death overlay.

### G. Scope and architecture

26. Inspect the diff: no out-of-scope features, no extra dependencies, no asset modifications beyond the supplied `imp` frames.
27. Confirm `src/game/systems` has no DOM/Canvas access, no wall-clock reads, and no randomness without an injectable seed; confirm rendering/input do not compute hits or death.
28. Confirm the programmer decision log documents tuning values, line-of-sight strategy, and any deviation.

## Acceptance criteria

QA records a **pass** only if:

- every automated check passes;
- every scenario above is executed and its result recorded;
- all design-specified values and states are present, or each mismatch is either fixed or explicitly approved in writing;
- no out-of-scope feature, dependency, or asset change exists;
- no blocking finding remains open.

Otherwise QA records **changes requested** with reproducible steps, expected versus actual behaviour, severity, and the responsible agent for each finding.

## Dependencies and handoff

Inputs: programmer implementation commit, approved design decisions, programmer decision log, committed assets.

Outputs: `qa/0.2.0/001-report.md` containing environment, commands, scenario results, screenshot references, findings, and a final pass/changes-requested verdict. Then hand back to the orchestrator.

## Open assumptions / unresolved items

- Test browser: the same desktop browser used by the programmer; record the exact version in the report.
- Manual verification is allowed for feel and visual feedback where automation would be brittle; automated checks remain mandatory.
- Headless Cypress throttles `requestAnimationFrame`; poll the debug snapshot instead of relying on wall-clock waits, and cover timing-sensitive rules in unit tests.
